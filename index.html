Inspiration from https://anydice.com/ and https://megadoug.com/dice-graph/

One entry per line. 1000r10{1,2,3,4,5,6} means 1000 rolls of 10 dice with six sides. Dice can be loaded. eg: 1000r10{1,2,3,4,5,6,6,6} is a six sided die where a roll of 6 is three times more likely. You can have multiple types of dice. Here is two Sicherman dice 2000r{1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8}{1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 4}. Here are three normal die and four 20 sided die 1000r3{1..6}4{1..20}. Note that 1..6 indicates a sequential range of sides, 1,2,3,4,5,6. Simple comment text (no dice codes, etc) can be placed at the end of the line. A display graph title can be added by: title"This is the name"
